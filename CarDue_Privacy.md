## 1. Who we are

CareDue (“**the App**”, “**we**”, “**us**”) is an offline-first maintenance diary for vehicles, home equipment (UPS, solar, RO, appliances), and related service logs.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles, why, and your choices. By installing or using CareDue, you agree to this policy.

## 3. Information the App processes

### 3.1 Information you enter (maintenance diary)

Examples of content you may create in the App:

- Asset names, types, notes, due dates, intervals, parts  
- Service logs (dates, costs, meters/odometers, actions, notes)  
- Vendors / contacts you choose to save  
- Money schedules (bills/EMIs), AMC contracts, document reminders  
- Photos / images you attach (receipts, equipment, cover photos)  
- App settings (language, region pack, reminder preferences, display options)

This content is stored **locally on your device** using an on-device database. We do not operate a CareDue cloud account that receives this diary content in the current version.

### 3.2 Device & app information (for ads, crash-adjacent SDKs, and OS features)

Depending on platform and permissions you grant, third parties or the OS may process:

- Advertising identifiers (e.g. Google Advertising ID on Android), IP address, device type, OS version  
- Approximate location derived by ad networks from IP (we do not ask for fine location for the diary)  
- Ad interaction and diagnostic data needed to serve and measure ads  

### 3.3 Permissions (Android)

CareDue may request:

| Permission / capability | Why |
|-------------------------|-----|
| Internet | Load ads; optional future online features |
| Notifications | Due / service reminders you enable |
| Camera / photos (picker) | Attach offline photos to logs, docs, covers |
| Exact alarms / schedule exact alarms (if used by OS APIs) | Reliable local reminders where required |

You can deny optional permissions; core offline logging is designed to keep working without ads/photos where possible.

## 4. How we use information

We use App data to:

1. Provide the offline diary, due calculations, history, export (CSV/PDF), and reminders  
2. Remember your preferences (language, packs, quieter ads / unlocks)  
3. Show advertisements via Google AdMob and enforce in-app ad policy caps  
4. Improve reliability and fix bugs (through standard store / OS crash reporting if you have it enabled)

We do **not** use your maintenance diary contents to train public AI models.

## 5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

CareDue is supported by ads. We use **Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)**.

Ad formats may include: banner, native, interstitial (after certain completed actions), rewarded (only after you tap), and app-open (cold start / long background), subject to in-app frequency limits.

Google and its partners may collect and process data as described in:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)  
- [How Google uses data from apps](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)  
- AdMob / advertising settings on your device  

**Your choices**

- Watch opt-in rewarded videos for temporary ad-free (“week pass”) when offered  
- Optional in-app purchase to remove ads permanently when offered  
- Reset / limit ad personalization in Android Settings → Google → Ads  
- Use system-level privacy controls available on your device  

If you are offline, ad requests fail gracefully; your diary remains usable.

## 6. Notifications & home screen widgets

**Notifications:** If you enable reminders, CareDue schedules **local notifications** on the device. Notification content reflects your assets and due items. You can disable reminders in App settings and/or system notification settings.

**Home screen widgets (Android):** Optional widgets (due glance, next due, quick actions) show summaries from your **local** CareDue data. Widget preferences are stored on your device. Widgets do not upload your diary to CareDue servers in the current version.

---

## 7. Photos & files

Photos you attach stay in the App’s local storage on the device unless **you** export / share them (e.g. share sheet, PDF/CSV you create). We do not upload diary photos to CareDue servers in the current version.

## 8. Children’s privacy

CareDue is a utility for home/vehicle maintenance and is **not directed at children under 13** (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child provided data, contact us and we will help you remove it from the device copy of the App where applicable.

The App is **not** designed for Google Play’s “Designed for Families” program.

## 11. Data retention & deletion

- **On-device diary data** remains until you delete assets/logs, clear App storage, or uninstall the App.  
- Uninstalling CareDue removes local App data from that device (subject to OS backups you may have enabled).  
- Ad partners retain data according to their own policies.  

To delete data: uninstall the App and/or clear CareDue storage in Android App info. For ad-related requests under applicable law, use Google’s tools and/or email us.
