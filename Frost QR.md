# FrostQR – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how *FrostQR* ("the App"), developed by *Reckap* ("we", "us", "our"), collects, uses and protects information when you use the App.

FrostQR is a utility app that scans and generates QR codes. It does *not* require you to create an account or provide any personal login details.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1. Information you provide

We do not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, or other contact information in order to use the core features of the App.

If you choose to contact us for support (for example, by email), we will receive the information you send (such as your email address and message contents). This information is used only to respond to your request.

### 1.2. Information processed on your device

The App processes the following information *locally on your device*:

- *Camera image frames* – used to detect and decode QR codes and barcodes. The live camera image is processed in real time on your device and is *not* sent to our servers.
- *QR content you generate* – text, URLs, Wi‑Fi credentials and other data you enter to generate QR codes. This data is stored locally on your device (for features like history) and is not transmitted to us.

### 1.3. Usage data & analytics

We may use third‑party services (such as Google services that come with the Play Store and AdMob) that collect certain usage data automatically, such as:

- Device information (model, OS version, language, country)
- App usage information (e.g. which screens are used, crash reports)
- Advertising identifiers (used for ad personalization/measurement, where enabled)

This data is generally aggregated and does not directly identify you. It is handled according to the respective third‑party privacy policies (see section 4).

---

## 2. Permissions

The App may request the following permissions:

- *Camera* – required to scan QR codes and barcodes using the device camera.
- *Photos/Media/Storage* – used to:
  - Select images from your gallery to scan QR codes.
  - Save generated QR codes as images to your device.
- *Internet & Network State* – required to load and display ads and to support core services from Google Play.

These permissions are only used to provide the corresponding features. You can revoke permissions at any time in your device settings, but some functionality may stop working.

---

## 3. How We Use Information

We use information for the following purposes:

- To provide core features of the App (scan/generate QR codes, history, saving/sharing QR images).
- To improve stability and performance (e.g. using crash reports and anonymous analytics).
- To show ads and measure their performance (see section 4).

We do *not*:

- Sell your personal data.
- Use your QR content for profiling.
- Upload your scanned QR images to our own servers.

---

## 4. Third‑Party Services

The App may use the following third‑party services:

- *Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)* – to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads.
- *Google Play services / Firebase‑related services* (if enabled by the Play Store environment).

These third parties may collect and process data in accordance with their own privacy policies, for purposes such as ad personalization, frequency capping, and measurement.

You can learn more here:

- Google’s Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Google AdMob: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

Depending on your region, Google may show a consent dialog (e.g. for GDPR/EEA) to manage your ad preferences.

---

## 5. Children’s Privacy

FrostQR is *not directed to children under 13* (or the equivalent age in your jurisdiction) and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete such information as soon as possible.

---

## 6. Data Retention

- QR history (scanned and generated codes) is stored *locally* on your device and can be cleared at any time from within the App.
- Logs and analytics collected by third‑party services are retained according to their respective policies.

If you uninstall the App, all locally stored data (including QR history and saved settings) will be removed from your device.

---

## 7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by the App. However, no method of storage or transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your continued use of the App after any changes means you accept the updated Policy.
